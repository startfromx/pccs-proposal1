# PCCS Client Search Questions

## Purpose

These are the questions a potential PCCS customer is likely to ask before starting a sportswear or apparel programme. They should become a future client-facing FAQ and content plan after PCCS confirms the answers.

## Company fit

1. What does PCCS manufacture?
2. Does PCCS work with sportswear brands, lifestyle brands, or both?
3. What product categories does PCCS specialise in?
4. Does PCCS work with new brands or only established brands?
5. What countries does PCCS operate in?
6. Which production location is best for my product and target market?

## Product development

7. Can PCCS develop a product from a sketch or reference garment?
8. Can PCCS help convert a concept into a technical pack?
9. Does PCCS provide pattern making and sample development?
10. Does PCCS support 3D fitting or digital product development?
11. Can PCCS help select fabrics, trims, labels, and embellishments?
12. What performance requirements can PCCS help engineer into a garment?
13. How many development rounds or samples are normally required?
14. How long does product development usually take?

## Manufacturing

15. What is the minimum order quantity?
16. What is the maximum production capacity for a programme?
17. Can PCCS support small runs, repeat orders, and large-scale production?
18. Can PCCS manufacture technical knitwear and performance fabrics?
19. Does PCCS provide printing, embroidery, sublimation, bonding, or heat transfer?
20. Does PCCS produce seamless garments?
21. Can PCCS support club, team, federation, or tournament apparel programmes?
22. How does PCCS manage production across different countries?

## Quality and compliance

23. How does PCCS control quality during production?
24. What testing and compliance requirements can PCCS support?
25. Which certifications are currently held, and what products or facilities do they cover?
26. How does PCCS protect brand marks, artwork, and technical information?
27. How does PCCS manage colour, fit, wash performance, and repeat-order consistency?
28. What happens if a product does not meet the agreed specification?

## Delivery and commercial planning

29. What information does PCCS need before providing a quotation?
30. How are development, material, tooling, and production costs calculated?
31. What are the normal lead times from approval to shipment?
32. Can PCCS help with material planning and production scheduling?
33. Which shipping markets and delivery terms can PCCS support?
34. How does PCCS manage urgent or seasonal orders?

## Sustainability

35. What sustainability data can PCCS provide for a product programme?
36. Can PCCS support recycled or preferred materials?
37. Which environmental, social, and chemical-management standards are relevant to PCCS products?
38. How does PCCS measure energy, emissions, water, waste, and materials?
39. Can PCCS provide current certificates and audit documents during the sourcing process?

## Working with PCCS

40. Who will manage my account and product development programme?
41. What is the best way to send a product brief to PCCS?
42. What information should be included in a first enquiry?
43. Can PCCS sign a confidentiality agreement before receiving technical information?
44. How can I request a factory, development, or capability discussion?
45. How do I contact PCCS for a quotation or partnership conversation?

## Highest-priority questions for the first FAQ

The first public FAQ should answer these questions because they directly affect whether a buyer makes an enquiry:

- Can PCCS develop a product from a sketch or reference garment?
- What sportswear and apparel categories does PCCS manufacture?
- What are PCCS minimum order quantities and production capacities?
- What is the usual development and production lead time?
- Which countries and facilities can support my programme?
- What quality and compliance standards can PCCS support?
- Can PCCS provide current sustainability certificates and data?
- How does PCCS protect brand marks and confidential product information?
- What should I include in my first enquiry?
- How do I contact PCCS for a capability discussion?

## Information PCCS should provide before publishing answers

- Minimum order quantities by product type and facility
- Typical development, sampling, and production lead times
- Current capacity by facility and product category
- Approved testing, compliance, and certification list
- Current sustainability data and certificate documents
- Confidentiality and intellectual-property process
- Enquiry contact, sales process, and response expectations
- Approved wording for customer, club, federation, or brand relationships
