Questions, answered

A clearer view
of PCCS.

What does PCCS do?
PCCS is an engineering-led group that designs, develops and manufactures technical sportswear, from fibre and fabric to the finished garment, mark and delivery.
What does “Engineering Future” mean?
It means treating every brief as a problem to solve precisely — combining design, materials, technical development, manufacturing, quality and delivery into one connected system.
Which parts of the product chain does PCCS cover?
The chain runs from concept and technical specification through fabric development, pattern and sample, trims and marks, testing and compliance, making and quality control, then finishing and delivery.
What does PCCS make besides apparel?
The wider group includes medical and healthcare activities through La Prima, financing and insurance activities through EZFY, and other group businesses described in the corporate structure.
Where does PCCS operate?
PCCS has an integrated operating footprint across Malaysia, China, Cambodia and Vietnam, with group activities and commercial links across the region.
How does PCCS approach quality?
Quality is built in from the cut, not added at the end. PCCS checks inline, during production and before shipment, with China serving China and Cambodia and Vietnam serving Western markets.
How much technical capability sits behind the work?
The approved capability summary records more than 50 patents, over 100 R&D staff, around 1,800 styles per year and approximately 67,000 samples per year.
What does a customer bring to PCCS?
A customer can bring a brief, sketch or reference. PCCS turns that starting point into a technical package, a factory-ready build and samples for approval.
Since when has PCCS reported sustainability?
PCCS has reported sustainability since FY2018 to Bursa Malaysia’s standard. The site’s sustainability figures are presented as reported, audited measures rather than aspirations.
Which sustainability measures are shown?
The current summary shows 4,659.20 tCO₂e of GHG emissions, 55,813.15 GJ of energy and 1.87 million kWh of renewable solar energy.
Which frameworks and certifications are referenced?
The current reference list includes GRS, RCS, OEKO-TEX, ZDHC, Higg FEM, WRAP Gold 2025 and the Cambodia Green Industry Awards 2025 (Silver), alongside reporting frameworks including GRI and the GHG Protocol.
How can I speak with PCCS?
For a conversation about capabilities, partnerships or the group, contact communication@pccsgroup.net.
