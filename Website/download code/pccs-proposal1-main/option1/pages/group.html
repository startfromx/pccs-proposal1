Our Story

From three sewing machines
to the world’s pitches.

Year Milestone
1973 Three second-hand sewing machines, Batu Pahat.
1981 Becomes Perusahaan Chan Choo Sing — PCCS.
1983 Adds embellishment — the mark begins.
1993–95 Forms as a group; lists on Bursa Malaysia (6068).
1998 First overseas manufacturing — Cambodia.
2005 China operations — PCCS Garments, Suzhou.
2015 Seamless garments capability (PSG, Cambodia).
2020 Adds medical arm — La Prima.
2024 Vietnam fabric mill — the last link in the chain.
Today Integrated maker across four countries. → Engineering Future.
