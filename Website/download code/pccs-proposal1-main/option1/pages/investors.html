Equity Story

From cut-and-sew to platform.

PCCS started as a cut-and-sew contractor. Over fifty years it moved steadily upstream — adding embellishment, then development, then fabric. Each link added is a link the customer no longer needs to source elsewhere.

The market still prices PCCS as a garment manufacturer. The business is becoming something different: an integrated platform that owns development, decoration and now materials. The gap between what is priced and what is owned is where the investment case lives.

股价之外，是我们更深的实力。这个差距，就是机会。
