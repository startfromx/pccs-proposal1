# PCCS Page Specifications - Initial Draft

## Home

- Position PCCS as an engineering-led group.
- Introduce Engineering Apparel and the wider group system.
- Show capability, recognition, work, sustainability, and next actions.

## The Engineering

- Explain the full chain from concept and fabric to quality, finishing, and delivery.
- Use the approved capability statistics from the final copy PDF only.

## Our Work

- Present the name-behind-the-names idea.
- Explain sectors served, marks, badges, and technical sportswear.

## Sustainability

- Present measured commitments and approved frameworks.
- Keep unresolved examples marked `NEEDS PCCS INPUT`.

## The Group

- Present the manifesto, story, leadership, and global footprint.

## Investors

- Use only approved investor copy and current approved data.
- Require CFO/compliance approval before publication.

## Careers

- Present engineering as the work of people across the group.
- Direct visitors to approved recruitment actions.
