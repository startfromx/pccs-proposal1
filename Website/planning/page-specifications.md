# PCCS Page Specifications - Initial Draft

## Home

- Position PCCS as an engineering-led group.
- Introduce Engineering Apparel and the wider group system.
- Show capability, recognition, work, sustainability, and next actions.

## The Engineering

- Explain the full chain from concept and fabric to quality, finishing, and delivery.
- Use the approved capability statistics from the final copy PDF only.

## Our Work

- Present the name-behind-the-names idea.
- Explain sectors served, marks, badges, and technical sportswear.

## Sustainability

- Present measured commitments and approved frameworks.
- Keep unresolved examples marked `NEEDS PCCS INPUT`.
- Keep a clear breathing space between the opening belief statement and the baseline section.
- Reported-measure figures should feel like a data band, not a full-width display headline; reduce scale when long values compete with the page hierarchy.
- Use a horizontal community examples / milestone-actions slider containing the reported FY2026 initiatives. Keep the annual-report totals visible: 10 initiatives, RM75,238 invested, and 24,702 beneficiaries. Use approved documentary images when available; until then, label concept visuals as illustrative and `NEEDS PCCS INPUT`.
- Keep the short “Giving back.” heading on one line where the responsive width permits.

## The Group

- Present the manifesto, story, leadership, and global footprint.

## Investors

- Use only approved investor copy and current approved data.
- Require CFO/compliance approval before publication.

## Careers

- Present engineering as the work of people across the group.
- Direct visitors to approved recruitment actions.

## Language versions

- Maintain separate English and Simplified Chinese entry pages for every route.
- English routes use the existing filenames; Chinese routes use the `-cn.html`
  suffix, for example `pages/sustainability.html` and
  `pages/sustainability-cn.html`.
- Language controls navigate between the paired files; they must not merge both
  languages into one content page.
- Shared navigation, hero behavior, footer, CSS, and JavaScript remain global.

## Implementation note

The Developer must compare each page against the final copy PDF before marking it complete.
