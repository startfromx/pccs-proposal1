# PCCS Image Style 01: Documentary Workplace Realism

Status: Approved style direction for testing

## Style intent

Real workplace photography taken during normal garment, textile, design, inspection, or production work. The image should feel like an authentic factory or studio documentation photograph, not a polished advertising campaign.

The premium quality should come from the subject, material detail, and believable observation—not from cinematic lighting or excessive retouching.

## PCCS facility calibration

When a factory scene is required, use PCCS's observed facility character as the reference: bright white and light-grey interiors, partitioned production bays, clean floors, white worktables, blue storage bins, large windows, practical fluorescent lighting, and workers in simple pink, grey, navy, or dark workwear. The factory should feel organized, efficient, and well maintained—not gritty, dark, greasy, or industrial for its own sake.

## Master prompt

Authentic workplace documentation photography for a global apparel engineering and manufacturing group. A real working moment captured during normal garment, textile, design, inspection, or production work. Natural human behaviour, focused working gestures, believable posture, ordinary clothing, realistic skin texture, visible fabric and machine detail. Practical factory, studio, showroom, or design-room environment with useful background context and minor everyday clutter. Mixed fluorescent light and daylight, uneven but believable exposure, mild colour cast, moderate sharpness, subtle camera noise, slight softness, natural depth of field, imperfect framing, and restrained colour grading. The image should feel like a real photograph taken by an in-house team or trade publication photographer: practical, human, observational, tactile, and quietly credible.

## Negative prompt

AI-generated appearance, cinematic advertising photograph, fashion campaign, polished stock photo, staged pose, eye contact with camera, artificial smile, perfect symmetry, perfect composition, studio lighting, dramatic shadows, glossy retouching, plastic skin, overly sharp detail, excessive bokeh, neon colours, futuristic factory, pristine showroom, empty background, repeated machinery patterns, duplicated people, distorted hands, extra fingers, fused fingers, unnatural eyes, impossible fabric, fake reflections, invented logos, readable text, watermark.

## Image rules

- Show one clear working action rather than a complicated scene.
- Keep ordinary clutter, partial obstructions, cropped equipment, and background activity when believable.
- Allow imperfect framing and slightly inconvenient camera positions.
- Use practical lighting and realistic mixed colour temperatures.
- Prefer moderate sharpness over hyper-detail.
- Keep skin, hair, clothing, machines, and fabric physically believable.
- Avoid making every image dramatic or cinematic.
- Do not add logos or readable text unless exact source artwork is supplied.
- Use muted industrial colours; avoid strong red or green backgrounds.
- For website crops, create usable space through the environment—not by making the scene look unnaturally empty.

## Preferred subject areas

- Sewing and garment construction
- Fabric, thread, seam, and material inspection
- Production machinery and process control
- Pattern fitting and measurement
- Design review and sample development
- Quality control and testing
- Real team interaction during work

## Prompt assembly template

Use the master prompt first, then add:

```text
Subject/action: [one specific working action]
Environment: [real PCCS workplace or believable equivalent]
Framing: [close detail, medium interaction, or wide process scene]
Lighting: [available factory light, window light, or mixed practical light]
Colour/materials: [specific fabric, machine, or garment details]
Website use: [hero, section image, card, or background]
```

## Reference basis

This style was calibrated against the supplied PCCS reference photographs showing fabric selection, material testing, production machinery, design collaboration, sewing, and fitting. The strongest qualities to preserve are their ordinary working moments, practical light, environmental clutter, imperfect framing, and process-led subject matter.
