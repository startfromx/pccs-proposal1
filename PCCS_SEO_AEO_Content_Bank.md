### Company identity and history

- PCCS Group Berhad is a Malaysian apparel group listed in Kuala Lumpur.
- The business story begins in 1973 with three second-hand sewing machines.
- The apparel business was formalised in 1981 under Perusahaan Chan Choo Sing Sdn. Bhd.
- PCCS Group Berhad was formed in 1993 and listed in Kuala Lumpur in 1995.
- The group expanded its manufacturing and operating footprint across Malaysia, China, Cambodia, and Vietnam.

### Apparel and technical capability

- PCCS presents itself as an engineering-led apparel and technical sportswear group.
- The company profile describes design and development work for functional and technical knitwear for sports.
- Product and sports categories shown in the company material include yoga and training, running, cycling, badminton, table tennis, football, and related performance apparel.
- Design principles presented in the material are “Look Better”, “Fit Better”, and “Sell Better”.
- The group describes development capabilities including 3D fitting, Browzwear, digital data platforms, sample development, and technical product development.

### Manufacturing and production network

- Company materials show manufacturing or operating locations in Malaysia, China, Cambodia, and Vietnam, with corporate and other offices in locations including Hong Kong and Singapore.
- The profile identifies apparel manufacturing, fabric production, printing, embroidery, embellishment, and seamless garment capabilities.
- The profile lists specialist processes such as laser perforation, eyelet, bonding, heat transfer, printing, embroidery, and sublimation.

### Quality, delivery, and sustainability language

- The company profile identifies world-class service, sustainability, product quality, and on-time delivery as commitments.
- The profile refers to clean energy, green electricity, renewable materials, and selected certifications or verification programmes.
- Certification and verification references in the profile include GRS, RCS, OEKO-TEX, ILO-BFC, ZDHC-related testing or programmes, and Higg FEM. Each reference must be checked against the current certificate scope and validity before being presented as a current claim.
